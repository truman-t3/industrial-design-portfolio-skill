## Portfolio-level structure

Use this default sequence for a full portfolio:

1. Identity and thesis
2. Selected work index
3. Flagship case study
4. Contrasting case study
5. Short supporting project or process appendix
6. Capabilities, role clarity, contact

The first project should prove the capability most relevant to the audience, not simply be the newest project.

## Single-project arc

Use an evidence-driven arc rather than a chronological diary:

1. **Hook** — final tension, product promise, or meaningful constraint.
2. **Brief** — user, context, objective, role, timeline, constraints.
3. **Evidence** — observations, research, benchmarks, or existing-product failures.
4. **Insight** — the design implication distilled from evidence.
5. **Divergence** — meaningfully different concepts, not cosmetic variants.
6. **Decision** — selection criteria, trade-offs, and chosen direction.
7. **Development** — form, CMF, ergonomics, architecture, interaction.
8. **Reality check** — prototype, test, manufacturing, cost, safety, or maintenance.
9. **Iteration** — what changed and why.
10. **Resolution** — final experience, product views, specification intent.
11. **Reflection** — outcome, limits, next validation step, personal learning.

Do not force every project through all eleven beats. Omit unsupported sections and explain gaps honestly.

## Multi-project selection

Build a capability matrix before choosing projects:

| Capability | Project A | Project B | Project C |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Research and insight | Strong | Medium | Low |
| Form and CMF | Strong | Strong | Medium |
| Engineering collaboration | Medium | Strong | Low |
| Prototyping and testing | Strong | Medium | Strong |
| Manufacturing awareness | Medium | Strong | Low |
| Storytelling and leadership | Strong | Medium | Medium |

Keep the smallest set that covers the desired role. Flagship projects need depth; supporting projects may use 3-6 pages.

## Page budgeting

| Format | Typical pages | Guidance |
|---|---:|---|
| Interview cut | 8-15 | One flagship project, fast proof |
| Single case | 12-18 | Full decision chain |
| Student application | 20-32 | 2-4 projects plus process range |
| Senior job portfolio | 18-30 | Role clarity, impact, collaboration |
| Client capability deck | 12-24 | Outcomes, process, risk control |

Reduce pages by removing repetition, not evidence.
