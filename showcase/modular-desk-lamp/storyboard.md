# Modular Desk Lamp — Fictional Showcase Storyboard

> Demonstration project only. All product details, observations, measurements, and outcomes are fictional placeholders for showing the portfolio workflow. They are not user research or engineering evidence.

## Case thesis

For people who shift between focused work and video calls in compact home workspaces, this concept explores a repairable task light whose head, arm, and base can be reconfigured without replacing the whole product. The proposition remains to validate with direct user research, prototypes, photometric testing, and engineering review.
